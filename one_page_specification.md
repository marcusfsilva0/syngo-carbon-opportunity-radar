# Enterprise Imaging Opportunity Radar – AI-Powered Market Intelligence

**Problem.** Identifying potential syngo Carbon opportunities requires continuous monitoring of public procurement notices and healthcare developments that signal enterprise imaging investment. Information is fragmented across federal procurement databases, industry publications, and health-system announcements. Manual research can miss formal buying events or earlier signals that emerge before an RFP.

**Who has the problem.** Product Marketing, Sales Enablement, and regional sales leadership responsible for the syngo Carbon Enterprise Imaging portfolio at Siemens Healthineers North America.

**What the artifact will do.** Enterprise Imaging Opportunity Radar is a market-intelligence prototype that searches selected public sources for (1) active solicitations and RFI/sources-sought notices and (2) earlier buying signals such as PACS replacement, enterprise imaging modernization, VNA procurement, data consolidation, and interoperability initiatives. It classifies findings by procurement stage, captures publication dates and response deadlines where available, screens for alignment with documented syngo Carbon themes, explains its matching evidence, identifies qualification gaps, and provides source links. It supports filtering and CSV export. Optional LLM analysis provides a second-pass interpretation grounded in a curated profile from supplied brochures.

**What it will deliberately not do.** The agent will not determine technical compliance, bid eligibility, commercial commitments, customer intent, account ownership, or win probability. It will not contact customers, update CRM, or access confidential Siemens/customer information. A third-party summary is never treated as the authoritative solicitation. Human validation against the issuing authority's original notice, attachments, and approved product/technical materials is required.

**How success will be measured.** Testing will include a current VA radiology/cardiology enterprise imaging solicitation, a New Jersey RFI whose response deadline has passed, unrelated healthcare procurement, PACS modernization announcements without an RFP, competitor announcements, duplicate coverage, and generic AI/digital-transformation news without imaging buying intent. Measures include correct signal classification, deadline/status handling, portfolio-theme precision, source traceability, and ability to flag unsupported assumptions. Success means reducing manual search effort while producing credible, traceable findings for human review.

**Scope and limitations.** Initial version: North America, English-language public sources, user-triggered scans over 7/14/30 days. Direct federal opportunity search uses the SAM.gov public API (user-supplied key); broader news discovery uses Google News RSS. State, provincial, private-hospital, and paid procurement databases are not comprehensively covered. The brochure-derived matching profile is a screening taxonomy, not product qualification or approved external positioning.
